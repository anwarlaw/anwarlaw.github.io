Welcome to Anwar Law Associates

Founded in 1978, Anwar Law Associates has proudly served clients for nearly five decades under the leadership of Advocate Muhammad Anwar, an LL.B. (Honours) graduate of Punjab University Law College. Throughout his distinguished career, he has represented clients from trial courts to the Supreme Court of Pakistan and has earned a reputation for integrity, excellence and dedicated advocacy. As former President of the District Bar Association, Toba Tek Singh, he has also mentored more than 70 junior lawyers, many of whom now serve the legal profession with distinction. Today, the firm continues its legacy by providing trusted legal services across Pakistan.

Our Practice Areas

We provide comprehensive legal services before Civil Courts, Criminal Courts, Banking Courts, Family Courts, Revenue Authorities and the High Courts throughout Pakistan.

Civil Litigation

Representation in civil suits, declarations, injunctions, recovery matters and property disputes.

Criminal Law

Bail matters, FIRs, trials, appeals, revisions and criminal defence before all courts.

Family Law

Divorce, Khula, maintenance, child custody, guardianship and inheritance matters.

Property & Revenue Matters

Sale, purchase, mutation, inheritance, partition, revenue appeals and land disputes.

Banking & Financial Litigation

Bank recovery suits, loan disputes, financial claims and banking litigation.

Corporate & Commercial Law

Business agreements, company matters, legal notices and commercial disputes.

Constitutional Petitions

Writ petitions before the High Court for protection of constitutional and legal rights.

Family & Succession Law

Khula, divorce, maintenance, child custody, guardianship and inheritance matters.

Civil & Criminal Appeals

Appeals, revisions and legal remedies before appellate and higher courts.
