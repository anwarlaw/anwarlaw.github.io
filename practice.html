Our Practice Areas

For more than five decades, Anwar Law Associates has represented individuals, businesses and institutions before Civil Courts, Criminal Courts, Banking Courts, Family Courts, Revenue Authorities, Tribunals, the High Courts and the Supreme Court of Pakistan.

Civil Litigation

Civil suits, declarations, injunctions, damages, recovery suits, contracts and legal disputes.

Criminal Law

FIRs, bail, trials, criminal defence, appeals, revisions and acquittals.

Property & Revenue

Mutation, registry, inheritance, partition, land ownership and revenue appeals.

Family Law

Khula, divorce, maintenance, child custody, guardianship and family disputes.

Inheritance & Succession

Succession certificates, probate, wills, legal heirship and inheritance matters.

Banking Litigation

Loan recovery, financial claims, banking disputes and Banking Court litigation.

Corporate & Commercial

Business agreements, company matters, partnership disputes and legal advisory.

Constitutional Petitions

Writ petitions before the High Court for enforcement of constitutional rights.

Appeals & Revisions

Civil and criminal appeals, revisions and remedies before appellate courts.

FIA & Cyber Crime

PECA offences, cyber fraud, online harassment and FIA investigations.

Tax & FBR Matters

Income Tax, Sales Tax, Customs, FBR notices and tax litigation.

NAB & Anti-Corruption

Representation before NAB, Anti-Corruption Establishment and inquiry authorities.

Service & Labour Law

Employment disputes, departmental proceedings, labour courts and tribunals.

Consumer Protection

Consumer court litigation, compensation claims and defective product disputes.

Arbitration & ADR

Alternative dispute resolution, mediation, arbitration and negotiated settlements.

Legal Drafting

Contracts, agreements, sale deeds, legal notices, powers of attorney and affidavits.

Registrar & Cooperative Matters

Registrar proceedings, cooperative societies and administrative litigation.

Legal Consultation

Professional legal opinions, due diligence, litigation strategy and legal advisory services.
